Iconic Sightseeing

Colorado National Monument

📍 Fruita / Grand Junction | 🚗 15 mins from downtown GJ

Experience sheer-walled, red rock canyons along the 23-mile Rim Rock Drive. Watch for bighorn sheep and soaring eagles. Stop at the Independence Monument view point.

Grand Mesa & Mesa Lakes

📍 Grand Mesa National Forest | 🚗 45 mins from Palisade

Escape to the largest flat-topped mountain in the world. Features over 300 alpine lakes perfect for fishing, paddleboarding, and brisk, scenic hikes among the pines.

Palisade Wine Country

📍 Palisade, CO | 🚲 Bikeable routes

Colorado's peach and wine capital. Rent a cruiser bike and ride along the Fruit & Wine Byway, taking in the dramatic Book Cliffs right behind the vineyards.

Getting Around

✈️ Air & 🚂 Train Travel

Grand Junction Regional Airport (GJT): Daily flights connecting through Denver, Dallas, and Phoenix. Rent a car directly at the terminal for easiest access to the Mesa and Monument.

Amtrak California Zephyr: Stops directly in downtown Grand Junction. One of the most scenic train routes in the US, traveling right alongside the Colorado River.


🚌 Local Transit (GVT) & Biking

Grand Valley Transit (GVT): Operates bus routes connecting Palisade, Grand Junction, and Fruita. Perfect for getting between downtowns if you aren't driving. Route 11 connects GJ to Fruita, and Route 4 goes to Palisade.

Riverfront Trail: A paved path connecting Palisade all the way to Fruita along the Colorado River. Highly recommended for safe, scenic travel between towns via E-bike.
